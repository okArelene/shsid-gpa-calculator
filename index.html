How the numbers work

01

SHSID weighted

Each semester, adjusted grade points are multiplied by course credits, added together, and divided by the entered credits. Cumulative mode calculates each semester separately, then averages the entered semester GPAs.

How credits are assigned. The local credit table follows the official student scheduling guideline: a course’s credits are the sum of its weekly class meetings. Each meeting under 55 minutes—including a standard 40-minute period—counts as 1 credit; each meeting lasting 55 minutes or longer counts as 1.5 credits. For example, four 40-minute meetings plus one hour-long meeting equal 5.5 credits.

See a credit that does not match your schedule? Message the developer on WeChat at unbased.

02

US unweighted

Unweighted GPA policies vary by school. This calculator removes plus and minus modifiers before assigning grade points: A = 4, B = 3, C = 2, D = 1, F = 0. Every entered course counts equally within a semester; cumulative mode then averages the entered semester GPAs.

03

UC estimate

Honors points raise the weighted UC GPA. Grade 10–11 semester grades marked * A–G count automatically, and eligible AP or IB courses earn one honors point for each A, B, or C semester grade. The capped calculation allows up to 8 honors points across Grades 10–11, including no more than 4 from Grade 10; the calculator adds them automatically.

Check your range. Compare your cumulative Grade 10–11 UC capped weighted GPA with the Admit GPA range in UC’s freshman admission dashboard. Select the campus or school and major or discipline that match your search. Use the result as context, not as an admission prediction.
