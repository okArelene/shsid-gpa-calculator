Mode

Method

Three numbers, three rules.

01

SHSID weighted

Each semester uses the original course credits and level adjustments. Cumulative mode averages the entered semester GPAs.

02

US unweighted

A = 4, B = 3, C = 2, D = 1, F = 0. Courses count equally within each semester; semester GPAs are then averaged.

03

UC estimate

Grade 10–11 semester grades marked * A–G count automatically. AP and IB levels receive eligible international honors points; school H/H+ and A-Level do not.

Official UC rule ↗

No manual A–G switch. The indicator comes from the local course catalog, and UC honors treatment follows the selected level.
